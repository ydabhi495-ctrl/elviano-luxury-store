About Elviano

Elviano is a premium fashion brand designed for elegance, confidence, and timeless style.

Founded by Idris Kapadia in a converted textile mill, Elviano draws on four generations of family tailoring. Each garment leaves the atelier signed in indigo thread by the cutter who shaped it — a small, permanent mark of accountability.

“A good suit is architecture
you can walk inside.”
— Idris Kapadia, Founder
